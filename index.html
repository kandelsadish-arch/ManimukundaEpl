Week 1 Activity

Community Clean-up Campaign and Awareness Program

Published: October 1, 2026

Our opening week kicked off with massive energy as over fifty dedicated volunteers gathered for a comprehensive community clean-up initiative. Armed with reusable gloves, biodegradable bags, and an unwavering commitment to the environment, the team targeted high-traffic public zones prone to plastic pollution.

Throughout the morning, participants systematically cleared discarded plastics, glass bottles, and non-biodegradable waste. Beyond clearing the physical debris, the campaign served as an educational platform. Volunteers engaged directly with local residents, sharing practical insights on waste segregation at the source and implementing daily habits to significantly reduce individual environmental impact.

The event concluded with a successful waste sorting session, ensuring that recyclable items could be properly managed. This powerful launch set a high standard for the upcoming weeks, demonstrating that collective community action can create immediate positive change.

Week 2 Activity

Tree Plantation and Trash to Treasure hunt

Published: October 8, 2026

Week 2 focused deeply on green infrastructure restoration. EPL MMC members and local youth groups joined forces to promote tree plantation and environmental responsibility.

Participants learned about proper planting methods, long-term care, and the importance of protecting native plants and trees.

The Trash to Treasure activity also encouraged students to transform discarded and reusable materials into useful and creative products. The activity demonstrated that waste can have value when managed creatively.

Week 3 Activity

Role Play Act and School Awareness Program

Published: October 15, 2026

As part of the Environment Premier League (EPL), Manimukunda College organized an environmental role-play activity to make environmental education more engaging and meaningful.

Through creative performances, students presented important environmental issues and demonstrated how everyday actions can affect our surroundings.

The activity encouraged students and community members to think about their responsibilities toward nature and inspired them to take positive environmental action.

Week 4 Activity

Ghardailo Program and Dustbin installation

Published: October 22, 2026

In the fourth week of the Environment Premier League (EPL), Manimukunda College conducted a Ghar Dailo awareness program and installed dustbins in selected areas. Students visited households to spread awareness about proper waste management, waste segregation, cleanliness, and reducing littering. The installation of dustbins provided a convenient way for people to dispose of waste properly and helped promote a cleaner, healthier, and more responsible community.

Our goal: To turn environmental awareness into responsible action and create a cleaner and healthier community.

About EPL MMC

The Environment Premier League (EPL MMC) is an environmental initiative associated with MainaDevi Foundation that promotes environmental awareness, cleanliness, community participation, and responsible action.

Through structured weekly activities such as community clean-ups, tree plantation, creative reuse, role plays, and awareness programs, EPL MMC encourages students and communities to become active environmental stewards.

The goal of Manimukunda College in the Environment Premier League (EPL) is to create environmental awareness and encourage people to take action for a cleaner and healthier community. Through activities such as clean-up campaigns, awareness programs, waste management, Trash to Treasure, Ghar Dailo programs, and dustbin installation, we aim to reduce pollution and promote responsible environmental habits. Our efforts can help reduce litter, improve waste management, protect nature, and inspire others to become responsible environmental citizens.

Why Manimukunda College Should Win EPL

Manimukunda College deserves to win the Environment Premier League because we have focused not only on competition but on creating meaningful environmental change. Our team has actively participated in awareness programs, waste management, creative reuse, community visits, cleanliness activities, and dustbin installation. We have worked to turn environmental awareness into real action and involve the community in protecting our surroundings. Winning the EPL would recognize our teamwork, creativity, dedication, and commitment to building a cleaner, greener, and more sustainable community.
